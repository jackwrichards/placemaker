## Name

Placemaker

## Short description

Select an area and turn it into roads, parking, parks, shops, nature, farms or sports: 25 types, over 100 styles, every setting adjustable. Preview it, then build it.

## Long description

Placemaker fills an area for you. Select some land, choose what you want there, check the preview and build it.

[Watch the showcase video on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ghIR5UhFwj4) (3 minutes).

**Not just presets.** There are 25 types and over 100 styles to start from, and each one has its own settings: layout, paths, planting, surfaces, extras. Change anything and save it as your own style. That makes billions of combinations; the pictures show only a few.

It only uses the game's own assets, so no asset packs are needed.

**How to use**

1. Click the Placemaker button at the top left, or press Ctrl+Shift+A.
2. Select an area: click a city block, click corners, brush it on, or click the road nodes around it.
3. Choose a type and a style. The preview updates as you go.
4. Press Build. Undo works if you don't like it.

**What's in it**

- Roads: street grids connected to the roads around them
- Parking: lots of any shape, with trees, lamps and fences
- Parks: parks, gardens, memorial parks, playgrounds, amphitheatres
- Beach: sand painted from the water's edge, quays and boardwalks
- Shops & squares: strip malls, mall parks, mall districts, market squares
- Nature: wetlands, reserves, lookout hills, hiking trails, campgrounds
- Farms and Sports: farmsteads, animal parks, sports parks, golf, a skate spot

Most types have a few styles. Settings are under Options > Placemaker.

**Early version**

Roads, parking and parks are the most finished. Shops and squares still need work. Known issues:

- The strict grid and superblock road presets build nothing yet
- Big builds can freeze the game for a second or two
- Shops in plazas and market squares face the city street

If something looks wrong, please report it with a screenshot and your Placemaker.log (in the game's Logs folder).
