What this game's own premade types build on one standard site: 140 x 110 m, flat, a street along the south side (local metres, x east, z north, origin the south-west corner). They show scale, counts and structure that read well in this game. They are rough and they are the floor, not the ceiling: take the player's request and run with it, go further and be far more creative and complete than these. A farm should be a fully fleshed-out farm.
